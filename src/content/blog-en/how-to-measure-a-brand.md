Sales can be counted, clicks can be counted. But how do you measure an idea in people's heads? With a few simple measures, always read together.

## Awareness

Measured with a survey of the audience that matters to you:

| Type | Question | What it tells you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Unaided** | "Which tea shops do you know?" | How easily the brand comes to mind on its own |
| **Aided** | "Which of these do you know?" | How well the brand is recognised |

Aided awareness grows first; unaided is harder to move, but it's what counts when someone has to choose.

## NPS

You ask: *"How likely are you to recommend this brand to a friend, from 0 to 10?"*

- **Promoters**: 9 and 10.
- **Passives**: 7 and 8.
- **Detractors**: 0 to 6.

**NPS = % promoters − % detractors**. It ranges from −100 to +100.

## Branded searches

How many people search for the brand name on Google, month by month (via Google Trends, Search Console or a keyword planner). They're an indirect awareness signal, but often **seasonal**: they rise and fall in the same months every year.

## Reading without panicking

At the fictional tea shop Cinque Foglie, two worrying numbers arrived in October:

1. **Branded searches fell 18%** compared with September. But the same happened in 2025 (−16%), and compared with October 2025 they're 22% higher. **False alarm**: it's seasonal.
2. **NPS fell from 52 to 31.** Detractors' answers explain why: the website promises 24-hour delivery, but since October orders take 4 days. **Real problem**: a broken promise.

A brand is a promise kept: when communication promises more than the company delivers, the disappointment hits the brand.
