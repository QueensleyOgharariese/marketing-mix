A social account isn't a shop window: it's a conversation. Comments and messages arrive every day, and how you answer says more about the brand than any post.

## Priorities

1. **Public problems that have been waiting** (a late order, a faulty product).
2. **Personal data in public** (addresses, order numbers, phone numbers).
3. **Questions** (quantities, shipping, availability).
4. **Compliments** and positive messages.

## What to hide and what not

| Comment | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Spam or scam | Report or hide |
| Insult with no content | Hide |
| Personal data | Hide and invite to a private chat |
| Honest criticism, even harsh | **Keep it** and reply |
| Partnership proposal | Reply and pass it to whoever handles it |

## How to reply to criticism

A good reply acknowledges the problem, apologises without blaming others, moves the details to a private chat and says what happens next.

> Hi Marta, we're really sorry about the wait and the unanswered emails. We'll message you privately right now to find your parcel and sort everything out.

If someone posts personal data publicly, don't repeat it in your reply: hide the comment and invite them to continue privately. It's respect for the person, and in line with the GDPR.

## Repeated questions

If three people ask the same thing in a week, many more are wondering silently. Answer each one, then turn the question into content (a carousel, a guide).

## A small crisis

In the fictional Cinque Foglie shop, dozens of kits arrived with a broken whisk and angry comments piled up. The steps:

1. **Get the facts** from the warehouse and customer service: how many orders, why.
2. **Pause scheduled posts** that would feel out of place, like a cheerful promotion.
3. **Publish an honest message**: what happened, apology, remedy, how to contact you.
4. **Reply to everyone**, personally, without hiding criticism.
5. **Contact people talking about it** with an audience (a creator, a journalist), with the facts.

How to talk to the press in these cases is explained in [crisis communication](/en/blog/crisis-communication/).
