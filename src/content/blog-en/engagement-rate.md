A profile with lots of followers isn't necessarily a profile with an attentive audience. To understand whether people really react, you use the **engagement rate**.

## What is engagement rate?

It's the percentage of people who interacted with a piece of content: they liked, commented, saved or shared it. It's one of the numbers used most by people working in social media and influencer marketing.

## How do you calculate it?

**Engagement rate = interactions ÷ base × 100**

The base can be:

- **reach**: how many people saw the post (usually the best choice);
- **followers**: how many people follow the profile.

They are two different measures: pick one and always use it when you compare.

## An example with numbers

A Cinque Foglie post with a matcha recipe:

| Interaction | Number |
| --- | --- |
| Likes | 180 |
| Comments | 25 |
| Saves | 15 |
| Shares | 5 |
| **Total** | **225** |

The post was seen by 5,000 people: 225 ÷ 5,000 × 100 = **4.5%**.

## Why there's no right value for everyone

Engagement rate changes a lot from one platform to another, from one format to another, and between small and large profiles: smaller profiles often have a more attentive community. That's why the most useful comparison is with **your own** posts, calculated the same way.
