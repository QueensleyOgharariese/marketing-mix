416|Think about the European rules on discounts.
417|Under the European Omnibus directive (in the Italian Consumer Code since 2023), anyone announcing a discount must show as the previous price the lowest one applied in the 30 days before the discount. That way you can't raise the price in October to fake a bigger discount in November.
418|A jacket cost €100 but for two weeks in October it was €80: the November discount is calculated from €80.
