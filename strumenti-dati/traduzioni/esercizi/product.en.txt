106|A launch is also prepared on the rules. Unauthorised health claims are banned (Reg. EC 1924/2006; for herbs many claims are still awaiting EU assessment). The "was" price of a discount must be the lowest of the last 30 days (Omnibus Directive): a new product doesn't have one. And generic, unprovable environmental claims are banned (EU Directive 2024/825).

127|Product marketing is the bridge between the product and the people who buy it. People in this role understand who a product is for, what makes it different, what price to sell it at and how to launch it, and they check that everything said about it is true and above board.
128|Features and benefits
129|The feature says what the product is like. The benefit says what changes in the life of the person using it. People buy benefits, but benefits must rest on true features.
130|An insulated bottle: "double-wall steel" is a feature; "your coffee stays hot until evening" is the benefit.
131|Positioning
132|It's an internal sentence, not a slogan: it says who the product is for, what need it meets and why it's different from the alternatives. Good positioning names a precise audience and only says provable things.
133|"For people who cycle to the office, the bottle that fits in the bottle cage and keeps drinks hot for 12 hours, unlike normal insulated bottles that are too wide."
134|Price and margin
135|The price is chosen by looking at three things: how much it costs to produce, how much similar competitors charge and how much value the customer perceives. The margin is what's left on each item: price minus cost.
136|The bottle costs €9 to produce, competitors sit between €20 and €30: at €24 the margin is 24 − 9 = €15.
137|A compliant launch
138|Before launching you check the label and the product page. In the EU you can't make unauthorised health claims for a food, show a discount against a price never charged, or say "green" or "zero impact" without proof.
139|On the bottle "BPA-free, certified" is fine if there's a certificate; "saves the planet" isn't, it's a vague claim.
