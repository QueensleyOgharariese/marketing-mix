117|CPC, CPA and ROAS
118|deciding with data
119|SEA (Search Engine Advertising) is the paid ads that appear on Google above or below the normal results, labelled "Sponsored". People in this role decide which searches the ads appear for, what to write in them and how much to spend, and then check with the numbers whether the spend brings in more than it costs.
120|You pay per click
121|The ad can appear a thousand times for free: you only pay when someone clicks it. The price of a click is called CPC (cost per click) and it's decided by an auction between everyone who wants to appear for the same search.
122|If a click costs €0.50 and you get 200 clicks in a month, you spend €100.
123|Keywords and negative keywords
124|Keywords are the searches you want to appear for. Negative keywords are the ones you DON'T want to appear for, because the people searching them won't buy. They stop you wasting money.
125|A teapot shop wants to appear for "cast iron teapot", but excludes "used cast iron teapot" and "how to clean a teapot": the people searching those don't want to buy a new one.
126|The ad
127|A good ad repeats what the person searched for, states a concrete benefit and has a call to action (order, discover, try). In Europe you can't promise a health effect for a food, or say "the best" without being able to prove it.
128|"Cast iron teapot 0.8 l | Keeps the heat for a long time | 24-hour shipping. Discover."
129|The numbers: CPA and ROAS
130|CPA is how much you spend to get one order (spend ÷ orders). ROAS is how much you take in for every euro spent (revenue ÷ spend). With ROAS below 1 you spend more than you take in.
131|You spend €100 and get 4 orders of €40: CPA = 100 ÷ 4 = €25; revenue = €160; ROAS = 160 ÷ 100 = 1.6.
