121|CRM (Customer Relationship Management) is the work of managing the relationship with existing customers and subscribers: splitting people into groups, writing at the right time with the right message and measuring results. All with people's consent, as the GDPR requires.
122|Segmentation
123|It means splitting customers into similar groups, to send each one the right message. A simple way looks at how many orders the person has placed and how long it's been since the last: new, loyal, inactive.
124|A candle shop: Elisa bought once yesterday (new); Tommaso buys every month (loyal); Rita hasn't bought for over a year (inactive).
125|Consent and rules
126|To send promotional emails you need the person's consent. There's an exception: people who already bought can be offered similar products, if they were informed and didn't object. Never bought lists, never addresses taken from social media, never email people who unsubscribed.
127|The candle shop can email people who signed up to the newsletter; it can't use emails collected by another company.
128|Automated emails
129|They go out on their own after something the person does, for example a cart left half-way. They work if they arrive soon, are few and remind people what was in the cart. Every email must have the unsubscribe link.
130|Abandoned candle cart: a reminder after 2 hours, maybe a second after a day, then stop.
131|CRM numbers
132|The open rate is emails opened ÷ emails sent. To estimate how much you recover from carts: abandoned carts × percentage recovered, then × average cart value.
133|5,000 emails sent, 1,000 opened: 20%. 200 carts, 15 in 100 recovered = 30, at €30 each = €900.
